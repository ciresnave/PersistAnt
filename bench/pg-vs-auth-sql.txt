What this says, and what it does not
- The two operations whose ratio is far from 1 are the ones where the layer issues MORE statements (2 against 1, and read-then-batch against one
  upsert). Their IQRs (0.12 and 0.24) sit well inside the gap from 1, even though control_same's IQR shows that single pairs are noisy here.
  The claim is the count and the ratio of about 2 and 2.9: it does not depend on a quiet box.
- Session read and kv get (one statement each side) are not separable from parity under this load: control_same's own spread (IQR 0.64) is
  larger than any gap they show. A quiet-box rerun would be needed to say anything finer than "about equal".
- NOT measured: concurrency (one connection, one op at a time), a request mix, tables larger than 2000 rows, TTL sweeps, list_user_tokens,
  swap_kv and the 25 other sites, auth-framework's own code (it is modelled by copying its SQL and call shape at 0cec4464, not run).
  The token's timestamps are SQL expressions (NOW() + interval), not bound parameters. Payloads: tokens about 600 bytes, sessions about 300.
- A possible reading for auth-framework (not measured here): a token check costing two round trips is a design choice of the index layer
  (kv.index); a secondary index in the same table, or caching the pointer, would be a different design. This run does not test either.
