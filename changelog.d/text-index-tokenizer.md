The helper that turns text into the `(term, frequency)` pairs `TextIndex::put` takes, behind the new cargo feature `text-index-tokenizer` of `persistant-kv` (root crate feature of the same name; it implies `text-index`). **New dependencies, only with the feature**, newest on crates.io at writing (PM ruling 2026-11-15): `unicode-segmentation` 1.13.3 (MSRV **1.85.0**, MIT OR Apache-2.0) and `unicode-normalization` 0.1.26 (MSRV **1.36**, (MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND Unicode-3.0, a term `deny.toml` already allows). **Effect on persistant's own MSRV: none**: both are below this workspace's 1.94, so it stays 1.94. `persistant-kv` without the feature pulls neither; `Cargo.lock` gains `unicode-segmentation` and two dependency edges (`unicode-normalization` was already locked).

`tokenize` does NFKC normalisation, lower-casing (not case folding: `Straße` stays `straße`), UAX #29 word boundaries (punctuation and white space dropped; `don't` and `3.14` stay whole; each Han ideograph is a word; a kana run is one word), and drops a word over 64 bytes; no stemming, stop words or language detection. The result is sorted by term with counts. Verified on a fixed corpus (words and counts, empty and punctuation-only input, apostrophes and numbers, composed versus decomposed é, full-width forms, the fi ligature, `Straße` versus `STRASSE` as a stated non-match, Han, katakana, mixed scripts, the 64-byte limit written out in the test and counted in bytes not characters) and end to end (text tokenised, put into a `TextIndex`, found by a query written composed, decomposed or full-width). Five mutations each fail a test: no NFKC, no lower-casing, a limit of 65, no length filter, a limit counted in characters.
