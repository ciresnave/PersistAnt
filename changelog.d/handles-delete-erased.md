kind: fix: `delete_erased` / `execute_erased` reach the registry's handles, bounded or not; blocking `KvHandle::delete_erased`, `BlobHandle::delete_erased`, `RelHandle::execute_erased`
---
0.18.12 added `delete_erased` and `execute_erased` to the backends, but the wrappers the registry builds did not forward them, so every call through a handle got the default `Unsupported`: `AnyKv`, `AnyBlob`, `AnyRel`, `persistant_sqlx::Bounded` (the call-bound wrapper) and the relational `Faulty` and `Traced` wrappers. They forward now, so `registry::open_named(..).kv().delete_erased(key)` works on `fs` and on SQLite (`sqlite`, `sqlite-full` including its WAL file, `sqlite-secure`), with or without a call bound; memory, PostgreSQL, MySQL and MariaDB still say `Unsupported`, honestly. The blocking facade gains `KvHandle::delete_erased`, `BlobHandle::delete_erased` and `RelHandle::execute_erased` (SageAnt's request, relayed by the PM).

Verified through the registry with raw-file read-back: a canary survives a plain delete on a WAL database (in the `-wal`, the control) and a file's bytes stay readable through a second hard link, and neither survives the erased call, async and blocking, bounded and not. Four mutations each fail a test: removing the forward in `AnyKv`, `AnyRel`, `AnyBlob` or `Bounded`. Not in this change: the batch form (`BatchOp::DeleteErased`, one atomic erased batch), which follows.
