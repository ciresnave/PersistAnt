kind: HOTFIX: `Lease::acquire` holds its claim for at least 10 s (BEHAVIOUR CHANGE); main was red on Windows
---
**BEHAVIOUR CHANGE in a locking primitive.** `Lease::acquire` claimed a key with the lease's own `ttl`, then took the fencing token from a counter and wrote it with a compare-and-swap. When the counter update outlasted a short `ttl` (a slow machine), the claim had lapsed, the swap found nothing and a key nobody held was refused. The claim is now held for at least 10 s and the real `ttl` is set by the write of the token, so a granted lease still lapses `ttl` after the grant. **Consequence: a caller that dies between the claim and the token write holds the key for up to 10 s, where before it held it for its `ttl`;** nobody else is granted it meanwhile, and it holds token 0.

Why a hotfix: the Windows jobs of CI failed on `lock.fencing` in `cargo test` and, on main at e69a416, the regenerated `lock.lease` row came back wrong on `SQLite on windows-latest` ("a free lease was not granted before a race round") against the checked-in yes; the probe's `ttl_ms=200` is the same 200 ms claim that lapsed, so the checked-in Windows row is earned only when the probe runs without that lapse, which this fix makes true. Two tests, each red with the claim floor at zero: a free key is granted when `get` outlasts the `ttl`; a dying caller holds the key for the floor and no longer. The per-run lease keys added to the conformance runner in 0.18.29 as a mitigation did not help and are removed.
